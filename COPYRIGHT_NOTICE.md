  - Substantial creative input
  - Original prompts and direction
  - Curation and selection
  - Editing and refinement
  
- ✅ **This qualifies** because you:
  - Created the premise and genre
  - Designed the world-building system
  - Directed the narrative flow
  - Selected and curated output

- Similar to photographer using a camera (tool vs. creator)
- AI is the tool; you are the author

## ✅ Enforcement Steps

### If Someone Infringes:

1. **Document Everything**
   - Screenshots
   - URLs
   - Timestamps
   - Archives (archive.org)

2. **Send DMCA Takedown** (if in US)
   ```
   Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice
   
   I am the copyright holder of [work].
   The infringing material is located at: [URL]
   I request immediate removal.
   
   [Your signature]
   ```

3. **Contact Platform**
   - Most platforms have copyright report forms
   - Provide evidence of your original work

4. **Legal Action** (if needed)
   - Cease and desist letter
   - Small claims court (< $10k)
   - Full lawsuit (for major infringement)
